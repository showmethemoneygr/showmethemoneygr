Only with service providers who process data on our behalf: Stripe (payments), Discord (community), Cloudflare and GitHub Pages (hosting). We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising by third parties.

Account and purchase records for as long as legally required for accounting purposes. Marketing emails until you unsubscribe. Technical logs for a short period only.

This site sets no tracking cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free, and local browser storage to remember your language choice, which is not a cookie and requires no consent.
